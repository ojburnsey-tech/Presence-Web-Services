# Calling playbook: Presence Web Services

How to find local businesses, call them legally, and turn calls into free audits and paid
work. The privacy policy (section 4) already describes this activity, so follow it exactly.

## Before the first call

- [ ] Business phone number live, shown on the website, and **not withheld** when you call.
- [ ] ICO data protection fee paid (you are now collecting prospects' details).
- [ ] Website redeployed from `main` with the latest privacy policy.
- [ ] A tracking sheet set up (columns below).
- [ ] A way to screen numbers against the **TPS** and **CTPS** registers (see "The rules").

## The rules (PECR and UK GDPR, in plain English)

| Rule                                                        | What it means for you                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Screen every number against TPS and CTPS before calling** | Sole traders and some partnerships register on the TPS; companies on the CTPS. You can't tell which from the number, so check both, unless the business has told you that you may call. Use a TPS/CTPS checking service (compare current prices; some charge per number). Re-check before calling again if a few weeks have passed. |
| **Never withhold your number**                              | Your caller ID must show.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Say who you are**                                         | Your name and "Presence Web Services" at the start. If asked, give the website and the address on it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Respect "don't call me"**                                 | Stop immediately, apologise, and add them to the do-not-contact list. Never call them again.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Emails**                                                  | Limited companies and LLPs: you may email them about your services, with an opt-out in every email. **Sole traders and partnerships: only with their consent.** If someone on a call says "email me", that is consent; note it in the sheet with the date.                                                                          |
| **Keep records tidy**                                       | Delete prospects within 12 months of last contact if nothing comes of it. Keep the do-not-contact list. This is what the privacy policy promises.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Honesty**                                                 | No exaggerated claims, no invented results, no pretending to be from Google. Fake urgency is a breach of consumer protection law.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

Fines for unlawful marketing calls run into six figures. Screening takes minutes.

## Finding the right businesses

Search Google Maps for "[trade] [town]", for example "plumber Lisburn", "hair salon
Newtownards", "café Bangor". Good prospects show at least one **visible, specific problem**:

- no website link, or a broken one;
- wrong or missing opening hours, or "temporarily closed" when they are open;
- fewer than about 10 reviews, or reviews with no replies;
- few or very old photos;
- a website that doesn't work well on a phone, has no phone number at the top, or a dead form.

Spend two minutes per business noting **one** problem. The specific problem is your opener.

Best first targets: trades, salons and barbers, cafés, beauty, physios and other local services
that depend on people finding them nearby.

## When to call

| Business type          | Best times                        |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Trades                 | 07:30 to 08:30, or 17:00 to 18:00 |
| Shops, salons, offices | 10:00 to 11:30, 14:00 to 16:00    |
| Cafés and restaurants  | 14:30 to 16:30 (never at lunch)   |

Walking in is often better than calling for shops, salons and cafés. Use the same script, at a
quiet time, and show the problem on your phone.

## The script

**Opener (under 20 seconds):**

> Hi, is that [name]? This is Oliver from Presence Web Services, I'm based in Belfast. I'll be
> quick. I was looking at [business]'s Google listing and noticed [specific problem, e.g. it says
> you're closed on Saturdays]. Is that right?

**If they engage:**

> That's exactly the kind of thing I fix for local businesses. I'm offering a free audit: I go
> through your website and Google profile the way a customer would, and send you a short written
> list of what to fix first. No obligation. Would that be useful?

**If yes:** confirm their name, best email and a good time to talk it through. Ask: "Is it OK if I
email that to you?" (this records consent for sole traders). Then either send them the form link
(`presencewebservices.co.uk/contact/`) or take the details yourself and log them.

**Close the call:**

> Great. You'll have it by [day]. I'll give you a quick ring after to go through it, if that suits.

## Common replies

| They say                   | You say                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "Not interested."          | "No problem at all. Would you like me to make sure we don't contact you again?" (Log it either way.)                                                                           |
| "We already have someone." | "That's good. If you ever want a second opinion, the audit's free. Thanks for your time."                                                                                      |
| "How much is it?"          | "Smaller fixes start from £249 and monthly care from £179, with no VAT added and no long contract. But the audit tells you whether you need anything at all, and that's free." |
| "Just send me an email."   | "Of course. What's the best address?" (Note the consent.)                                                                                                                      |
| "Is this Google?"          | "No, I'm an independent business in Belfast. I just help people with their Google listing and website."                                                                        |
| "Call me back later."      | "Sure, when's best?" Book it and call exactly then.                                                                                                                            |

**Voicemail:** leave one short message at most, then don't leave another:

> Hi, it's Oliver from Presence Web Services in Belfast. I noticed something on your Google
> listing you might want to fix. No sales pitch; give me a call back on [number] if you'd like me
> to explain. Thanks.

## Tracking sheet columns

`Date` · `Business` · `Contact name` · `Phone` · `Email` · `Where found` · `TPS/CTPS checked
(date)` · `Problem spotted` · `Outcome` · `Email consent (date)` · `Next step and date` ·
`Do not contact (Y/N)` · `Notes`

Review weekly. Delete rows with no enquiry after 12 months. Move do-not-contact businesses to a
separate tab and keep it.

## Weekly targets (adjust once you have real numbers)

- **Prepare:** 50 businesses researched and screened.
- **Contact:** 20 to 30 calls or walk-ins a day, on three or four days.
- **Deliver:** every audit within the promised time. The audit is your sales pitch: make it
  specific, visual (screenshots) and short.
- **Measure:** calls made, conversations, audits agreed, audits delivered, paid jobs. After two
  weeks you will know your own conversion rates, and which trades and towns respond best.

## After each paid job

1. Ask for a Google review (link from your own Business Profile) and permission to use a short
   testimonial.
2. Ask: "Do you know anyone else who'd find this useful?" Referrals are the strongest channel for
   a local service business.
